# Notational

The existing Note and Simplenote terms are defined in [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md).

## Language

**Account session**:
The Simplenote account associated with the local Notes, and whether those Notes can sync.
Signing out stops sync but retains the account association and its local Notes.

**Account switch**:
Replacing the current account session with a different Simplenote account.
Unsynced changes must sync to the old account or be explicitly discarded before the switch.
